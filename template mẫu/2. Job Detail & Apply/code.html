$120k - $160k location_on Remote / San Francisco schedule Full-time

The Role

We are looking for a visionary Senior Product Designer to lead the evolution of our core recruitment intelligence platform. You will bridge the gap between complex AI algorithms and human-centered career experiences, ensuring that every interaction feels intuitive, transparent, and empowering for both candidates and hiring managers.

assignment_turned_in Responsibilities

  • check_circle Drive the end-to-end design process, from conceptual wireframing to high-fidelity prototyping and engineering handoff.
  • check_circle Collaborate with data scientists to visualize complex AI-driven matching scores and talent insights.
  • check_circle Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design hypotheses and iterate based on real feedback.
  • check_circle Maintain and evolve our design system, ensuring visual consistency across our web and mobile applications.

verified_user Requirements

  • check_circle 5+ years of experience in product design, specifically within SaaS or AI-driven enterprise platforms.
  • check_circle A portfolio showcasing complex system design, clean aesthetics, and deep strategic thinking.
  • check_circle Expert proficiency in Figma, including advanced prototyping and component library management.
  • check_circle Strong communication skills with the ability to articulate design decisions to non-design stakeholders.
